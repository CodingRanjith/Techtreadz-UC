Visa Sponsorship FAQ

Answers to the most common questions about our visa sponsorship services to help you navigate your transition smoothly.

What visa sponsorship services do we offer?

We offer comprehensive visa sponsorship services including assistance with visa applications, employment sponsorship, legal and compliance advice, relocation support, career counseling, and work permit guidance.

Who is eligible for visa sponsorship?

Visa sponsorship is typically available to skilled workers, students, and sometimes interns who have received a job offer from a Belgian employer willing to sponsor their stay.

How long does the visa sponsorship process take?

The timeline can vary based on the visa category and individual circumstances but generally ranges from a few weeks to several months. We work to expedite the process wherever possible.

What documents are required for visa sponsorship?

Documents typically include a valid passport, job offer from a Belgian employer, proof of qualifications, and sometimes proof of language skills. Specific requirements may vary by visa type.

Can my family accompany me under a visa sponsorship?

Yes, most work visas allow you to bring dependents, including your spouse and children. Each family member must apply for their own dependent visa.

What happens if my employment ends while I am under visa sponsorship?

If your employment ends, it may affect your visa status. It is crucial to seek new sponsorship or consult with immigration services to explore other options to remain legally in Belgium.
